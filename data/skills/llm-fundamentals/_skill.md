How the models themselves behave: tokenisation and context economics, sampling,
prompting technique, structured output, embeddings and retrieval, plus enough
fine-tuning literacy to know when *not* to fine-tune.

Interviewers use this area to separate people who wire APIs together from people
who can debug why a system is producing bad output.
