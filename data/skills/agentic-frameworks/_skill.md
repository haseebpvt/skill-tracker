Building agents that plan, call tools, and recover from failure: LangGraph,
LangChain, the Model Context Protocol, and the orchestration patterns that sit
underneath them (state machines, retries, human-in-the-loop, multi-agent
handoff).

This is the skill every scanned JD names explicitly, usually in the first three
bullets, which is why it sits at the top of the order.
